About Sanctum
Fully offline, open-source HD wallet. All cryptography runs in your browser via ethers.js. Zero network requests.

Security guidelines
Cold storage: Download page, go offline, generate seed, write on paper, clear browser cache.
Passphrase: Acts as a 25th word — without it, even a correct seed cannot access your funds.
Solana*: Uses ed25519 (SLIP-0010), different from secp256k1 used by ETH/BTC.
